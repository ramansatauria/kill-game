# 🎮 **Kill Game** 💀

Welcome to **Kill Game**! A thrilling action-packed game where you have to **defeat enemies**, **survive** against waves of attackers, and **upgrade** your weapons and abilities to be the ultimate champion.

## 🚀 **Demo**

Check out the live version of the game:  
[**Play the Game**](https://killgame.netlify.app/)

## 🧩 **Features**

- 🏹 **Dynamic Gameplay**: Face different challenges as you progress through levels.
- 💥 **Power-ups & Weapons**: Upgrade your abilities to defeat enemies.
- 🔥 **Intense Action**: Fast-paced and thrilling combat mechanics.
- 🎮 **Responsive Design**: Play on any device, be it mobile, tablet, or desktop.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards**: Track your progress and challenge others to beat your score!

## 🛠️ **Installation**

<div style="background-color: #f9f9f9; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
  <p>To set up the project locally, follow these steps:</p>
  <ol>
    <li><b>Clone the repository:</b>
      <pre><code>git clone https://github.com/ramansatauria/kill-game.git</code></pre>
    </li>
    <li><b>Navigate to the project directory:</b>
      <pre><code>cd kill-game</code></pre>
    </li>
    <li><b>Install dependencies:</b>
      <pre><code>npm install</code></pre>
    </li>
    <li><b>Run the app locally:</b>
      <pre><code>npm start</code></pre>
    </li>
    <li>Visit <code>http://localhost:3000</code> in your browser to see the app.</li>
  </ol>
</div>
